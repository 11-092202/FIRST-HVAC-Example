Maintenance Tips

Simple Ways to Keep Your System Efficient

A dirty filter restricts airflow, forcing your system to work harder and use more energy. Check filters monthly and replace as needed, especially during heavy use seasons.
Furniture, rugs, and curtains covering supply and return vents can reduce airflow and efficiency. Keep vents clear for even heating and cooling.
A furnace tune-up before winter and an A/C tune-up before summer help catch small problems early and keep your equipment running efficiently.
A maintenance agreement bundles seasonal tune-ups with priority scheduling, helping extend the life of your equipment and avoid surprise repair costs.
